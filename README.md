# The AI Architect: Engineering Workflows in the Age of Agentic AI

> **Theme:** Moving from "Chatting with AI" to "Building with Agents."

---

## 📌 Course Overview
This course helps you upskill from basic AI use to understanding how agentic AI transforms workplace workflows. You will gain a practical foundation in AI concepts, explore real-world implications, and practice applying AI as a productivity partner.

---

## 🎒 Pre-Expedition: The First Aid Kit (Minimum Viable Knowledge)
Familiarize yourself with these 5 core building blocks before starting:
* **Agent:** A digital worker capable of reasoning and taking action.
* **Prompt:** Instructions acting as the "code" for the Agent's brain.
* **API:** The "hands" allowing an Agent to move data between systems.
* **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** Giving AI a manual/database to provide company-specific answers.
* **Human-in-the-Loop:** A deliberate checkpoint where a human approves an action.
