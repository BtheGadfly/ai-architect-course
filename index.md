## Course Overview

This course helps you upskill from basic AI use to understanding how agentic AI transforms workplace workflows. You will gain a practical foundation in AI concepts, explore real-world implications, and practice applying AI as a productivity partner[cite: 2].

---

### Pre-Expedition: The First Aid Kit (Minimum Viable Knowledge)

Before starting the expeditions, familiarize yourself with these 5 core building blocks[cite: 2]:

* **Agent:** A digital worker capable of reasoning and taking action, not just answering questions[cite: 2].
* **Prompt:** The specific set of instructions that act as the "code" for the Agent's brain[cite: 2].
* **API:** The "hands" allowing an Agent to talk to and move data between software systems[cite: 2].
* **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** Giving AI a manual/database to provide company-specific answers[cite: 2].
* **Human-in-the-Loop:** A deliberate checkpoint where a human approves an action before execution[cite: 2].
