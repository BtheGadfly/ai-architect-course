# Expedition 1: The New Enterprise Blueprint

**The Goal:** Move from managing silos to managing flow.

## Is Your Organization a City of Bunkers?
Many organizations operate like a city of sealed-off bunkers. Finance, Sales, and Ops work in isolation. As an AI Architect, you are installing a Digital Nervous System using Agentic AI to translate actions in real-time.
